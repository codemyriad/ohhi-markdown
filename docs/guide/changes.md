# What's new

The changes in each release, newest first. The version you are running is
in the Help menu, at the bottom.

## October 2026 — first run, join links, sharing and help

**Getting started**

- The first screen asks one thing — what do you have? — and offers a door for
  each: open a folder of notes, add a folder someone shared with you, or bring
  your vault from another device. The vault list says what each vault is and
  how much of it is shared.
- **Import from another app…** explains how notes from Notion, Apple Notes,
  Bear or Logseq come in.

**Sharing**

- Right-click a folder, **Share…**: the share sheet shows the relay as a card
  (does it answer?), checks the **Team secret** as you type (*works* or
  *doesn’t match this relay*), and names the first link for the person it is
  for. **Copy message** puts the link in a short sentence, ready to send.
- On a team setup, links are web addresses. Clicked, they open a page that
  says who sent it, what you get and whether the relay answers, then lets you
  add the folder in the desktop app or in the browser.
- Adding a shared folder says "Adding *folder*" while it arrives.

**Help is in the app**

- A **Help** menu (the question mark at the toolbar's right end): these
  guides, keyboard shortcuts, and what's new.
- **Report a problem…** copies a short report — your sentence plus the facts
  someone needs to help — for you to paste wherever you ask for help. It never
  includes what your notes say, and the app sends it nowhere by itself.
- On a team setup, an installed update's notice has a **What changed** button
  beside *Restart*.
